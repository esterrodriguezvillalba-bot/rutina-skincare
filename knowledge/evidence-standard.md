# SkinOS Evidence Standard

## Mission

Every recommendation in SkinOS must be traceable, evidence-based and transparent.

The objective is not simply to recommend products, but to explain why they are recommended, how strong the evidence is, and under which circumstances they should or should not be used.

---

# Sources of information

Every protocol may include information from one or more of these levels.

## Level 1

Manufacturer documentation

Examples

- Panasonic User Manual
- Panasonic Official App
- Official Product Instructions

These define:

- device modes
- compatible gels
- contraindications
- treatment frequency

---

## Level 2

Peer-reviewed scientific literature

Accepted sources

- PubMed
- JAMA Dermatology
- British Journal of Dermatology
- JAAD
- Nature
- Cochrane

---

## Level 3

Professional clinical consensus

Examples

- Dermatologist consensus
- Aesthetic medicine consensus
- International guidelines

---

## Level 4

SkinOS interpretation

Only generated when previous evidence exists.

Never contradicts Level 1 or Level 2.

---

# Evidence grades

A
Multiple randomized controlled trials or systematic reviews.

B
Controlled clinical studies.

C
Observational studies or limited clinical evidence.

D
Expert opinion or manufacturer recommendation only.

---

# Protocol philosophy

Safety first.

Evidence before trends.

Explain uncertainty.

Never exaggerate benefits.

Always state contraindications.

Always explain treatment frequency.

Every recommendation must include:

- Why
- Expected benefit
- Evidence grade
- Sources
- Compatible products
- Incompatible products
- Frequency
- Notes
